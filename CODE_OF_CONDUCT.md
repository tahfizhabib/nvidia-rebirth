# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome in the Nvidia Rebirth community, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that keep this a friendly, helpful and respectful space.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps build a good community:

- Being kind and patient with people of every experience level
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Staying focused on what is best for the community, not just for oneself

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, trolling or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- Discriminatory jokes or language
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission
- Spamming, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or sharing malicious files or links
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including the GitHub repository (issues, pull requests and discussions) and the project Discord server. It also applies when someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainers by messaging them on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/krpUAHkf5m) or by using the **Security** tab's private reporting on GitHub if the issue is sensitive. All reports are handled promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these steps when deciding the consequences of a violation:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what was wrong, with a request to change the behavior. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences if the behavior continues, including a set period without interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from the project's spaces for serious or repeated violations.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual or aggression toward groups of people.
